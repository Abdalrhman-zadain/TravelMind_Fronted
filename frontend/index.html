Essential Travel Tips

🌤️

Best Time to Visit

Spring and autumn offer the most pleasant weather across Petra, Amman, and Wadi Rum.

🎒

What to Pack

Bring comfortable shoes, sun protection, and light layers for cooler desert evenings.

🤝

Local Customs

Dress modestly at religious sites and enjoy the warmth of Jordanian hospitality.

💳

Money & Budget

The Jordanian Dinar is the local currency, and the Jordan Pass often saves money.

🚗

Getting Around

Renting a car offers flexibility, while JETT buses and city taxis cover the major routes.

📱

Stay Connected

Airport SIM cards and hotel Wi-Fi make it easy to stay online while traveling.
