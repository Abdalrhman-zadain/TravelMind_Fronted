Travel Smart

Essential Travel Tips

🌡️

Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offer the most pleasant weather. Summers are hot, especially in Wadi Rum and the Dead Sea area.

🎒

What to Pack

Comfortable walking shoes are a must for Petra. Bring sunscreen, a hat, and layers — desert nights can be surprisingly cool.

🤝

Local Customs

Jordanians are incredibly hospitable. Learn a few Arabic phrases, dress modestly at religious sites, and always accept tea when offered!

💳

Money & Budget

The Jordanian Dinar (JOD) is the currency. Credit cards are widely accepted in cities. The Jordan Pass saves money on entry fees and visa costs.

🚗

Getting Around

Rent a car for flexibility or use JETT buses between major cities. Taxis are affordable in Amman. The King's Highway is a scenic drive.

📱

Stay Connected

Buy a local SIM card at the airport for cheap data. Zain and Orange offer great tourist packages. Wi-Fi is available at most hotels and cafés.
